#Social Media Do’s and Don’ts
✅ Do’s

1. Think before you post
Always check if your content is appropriate and respectful.

2. Protect your privacy
Keep personal information (address, phone number, passwords) private.

3. Be respectful
Treat others kindly—just like you would in real life.

4. Use strong passwords
Make your accounts secure with unique and strong passwords.

5. Verify information before sharing
Avoid spreading fake news—check sources first.

6. Maintain a positive digital footprint
Remember: what you post stays online for a long time.

7. Engage responsibly
Comment and interact in a meaningful and polite way.



❌ Don’ts

1. Don’t share personal details publicly
This can lead to privacy risks or scams.

2. Don’t post offensive or harmful content
It can hurt others and damage your reputation.

3. Don’t believe everything you see
Not all online content is true.

4. Don’t engage in cyberbullying
Never harass, threaten, or insult others online.

5. Don’t overshare
Avoid posting too much about your personal life.

6. Don’t click suspicious links
They may contain viruses or scams.

7. Don’t use social media excessively
Balance your online and real-life activities.
