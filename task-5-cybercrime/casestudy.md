# Case Study: Catfishing on Social Media

## Introduction
Catfishing is a form of online deception where a person creates a fake identity on social media to trick others. It is often used to manipulate emotions, gain trust, or exploit victims.

---

## Case Scenario
Arjun, a high school student, received a friend request from someone named "Sara" on social media. They started chatting regularly, and Arjun began to trust her. "Sara" shared attractive photos and claimed to study at a nearby college.

After a few weeks, she asked Arjun for money, saying she had an emergency. Trusting her, Arjun sent the money. Later, he discovered that the profile was fake, and the person behind it had deceived him.

---

## Issues Identified
- Fake online identity
- Emotional manipulation
- Financial exploitation
- Lack of verification of online profiles
- Overtrusting strangers online

---

## Warning Signs of Catfishing
- Profile has very few photos or followers
- Photos look too perfect or like stock images
- Avoids video calls or face-to-face meetings
- Shares inconsistent or vague information
- Quickly builds emotional connection
- Requests money or personal information

---

## Do’s
- Verify profiles before trusting (check mutual friends, reverse image search)
- Be cautious when talking to strangers online
- Keep personal information private
- Report suspicious accounts
- Talk to a trusted person if something feels wrong

---

## Don’ts
- Do not send money to someone you haven’t met
- Do not share sensitive information (address, passwords, bank details)
- Do not blindly trust online relationships
- Do not ignore red flags
- Do not engage further if you feel uncomfortable

---

## Prevention Tips
- Use privacy settings on social media
- Enable two-factor authentication
- Stay informed about online scams
- Educate friends and family about digital safety

---

## Conclusion
Catfishing is a serious online threat that can cause emotional and financial harm. Awareness and caution are the best ways to stay safe. Always verify before trusting someone online.

---

## Key Takeaway
**"Don’t trust blindly—verify before you connect."**
