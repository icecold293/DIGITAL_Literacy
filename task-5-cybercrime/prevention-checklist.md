# Prevention Checklist: Safe Social Media Use

## Personal Safety
- [ ] Do not share personal information (address, phone number, passwords)
- [ ] Use strong and unique passwords for each account
- [ ] Enable two-factor authentication (2FA)
- [ ] Keep your devices secure with passwords or biometrics

---

## Account Security
- [ ] Set profiles to private when possible
- [ ] Regularly review privacy settings
- [ ] Log out from shared or public devices
- [ ] Avoid using public Wi-Fi for sensitive activities

---

## Awareness & Behavior
- [ ] Think before posting or sharing content
- [ ] Verify information before forwarding or reposting
- [ ] Be respectful in comments and messages
- [ ] Avoid oversharing personal life details

---

## Identifying Risks
- [ ] Watch for suspicious profiles (few followers, fake photos)
- [ ] Avoid clicking unknown or suspicious links
- [ ] Be cautious of messages asking for money or personal details
- [ ] Do not trust strangers easily online

---

## Handling Problems
- [ ] Block and report suspicious accounts
- [ ] Save evidence (screenshots) of harmful activity
- [ ] Inform a trusted adult/authority if needed
- [ ] Change passwords immediately if you suspect a breach

---

## Digital Well-being
- [ ] Limit screen time
- [ ] Take regular breaks from social media
- [ ] Focus on real-life interactions and activities

---

## Final Reminder
**"Stay Alert, Stay Safe Online."**
